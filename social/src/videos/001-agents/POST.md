## Sound

Pick from the app's own library when posting (a licensed track can't be
baked into the render). Something light at around 100 to 120 bpm; the cuts
land every 1.5 to 3 seconds, so most upbeat lo-fi or indie tracks fit.
Keep it low enough that it doesn't fight the captions.

## Before posting

- Watch it once on a phone with the sound off: every caption should be
  readable in its time.
- The first line of both captions states the beta; keep it there.
- Pin a comment with the link if the platform hides the bio link.
